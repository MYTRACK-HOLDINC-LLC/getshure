Refund Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026. How returns and refunds work for GETSHURE books.

1. Window

You may return an unused hardcover within 14 days of delivery for a full refund of the book price.

2. Condition

Books must be unused, unmarked, and in original condition suitable for resale. Damaged or clearly read copies may be refused.

3. Shipping costs

Original outbound shipping is refunded only if we made an error (wrong item, damaged in transit). Return shipping is paid by the customer unless we ask you to return a defective item.

4. How to start a return

Email contact@getshure.com with your order number and reason. We’ll reply with the return address and next steps.

5. Refund timing

Once we receive and inspect the return, refunds go back to the original payment method within about 5–10 business days (banks may take longer to show it).

6. Damaged or wrong item

If your book arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 7 days with photos. We’ll replace it or refund you — including return shipping if we ask you to send it back.
