2. What we collect

When you order a book we collect the information needed to fulfill it: name, shipping address, email, phone (if provided), and payment details processed by our payment provider. We also collect basic site analytics (pages viewed, device type) to improve the shop.

Order records are kept as required for tax and accounting. You may ask us to update or delete account-style data where the law allows.

Email contact@getshure.com to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data, or to opt out of marketing email (transactional order email still sends).
